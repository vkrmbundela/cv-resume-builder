Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 21, 2026

1. Data Processing Model

This app is local-first. Resume content is processed in your browser for editing, preview, and export.

2. Local Storage

Your resume data is saved in browser local storage on your device to support autosave and recovery.

3. No Server-Side CV Storage

This app does not upload or store your resume content on a backend server for normal usage.

4. Exports and Sharing

When you export PDF/DOC/LaTeX or backup JSON files, those files are created locally on your device. You control if and where you share them.

5. Third-Party Libraries

PDF export relies on client-side libraries loaded in the browser. Review and host dependencies appropriately before public deployment.

6. Security Expectations

Do not use shared/public computers for sensitive resume editing. Clear local data after use on non-personal devices.

7. Contact and Updates

Policy text may be updated as the project evolves. Review this page before public or production use.
